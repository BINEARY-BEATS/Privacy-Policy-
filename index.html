Effective Date: June 20, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Deep Clean application handles user data. The app is designed to operate fully on-device for file cleaning and storage analysis.

2. Data Access

The app may request access to photos, videos, and files stored on the device. This access is required to scan storage usage, detect large files, and identify duplicates.

3. Data Processing

All scanning and analysis are performed locally on the device. No personal files are uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external servers.

4. Data Sharing

The app does not share user files or personal media with any third parties. The app does not use backend servers or cloud storage.

5. Third-Party Services

This version of the app does not integrate analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services.

6. Permissions

Storage permissions are required to analyze and manage files on the device. Users may disable permissions at any time through system settings.

7. Security

Since all processing is performed locally, user data remains under full device control.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13, and no personal data is knowingly collected.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future releases. Any changes will be reflected in this page.
