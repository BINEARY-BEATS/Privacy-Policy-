01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Deep Clean application handles user data. Deep Clean is built to operate fully on-device for file cleaning and storage analysis — it does not require an internet connection to function, and no personal data is transmitted to the developer or any other party.

02Data access

The app may request access to photos, videos, and files stored on your device. This access is required to:

  • Scan overall storage usage
  • Detect unusually large files
  • Identify duplicate or similar files

The app only reads file metadata and content needed to perform these functions — it does not read files unrelated to cleaning and storage analysis.

03Data processing

All scanning and analysis are performed locally, on your device's processor. No personal files are uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external servers, and the app does not maintain any cloud copy of your data.

In short: if your device is offline, Deep Clean works exactly the same way.

04Data sharing

The app does not share user files or personal media with any third parties. It does not use backend servers, remote APIs, or cloud storage of any kind, so there is no channel through which your data could be shared even inadvertently.

05Third-party services

This version of the app does not integrate analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. If a future release adds any third-party service, this policy will be updated first to disclose what is collected and why, before that update reaches you.

06Permissions

Deep Clean requests the minimum permissions needed to do its job:

PermissionWhy it's needed
Storage / Files accessTo analyze space usage and manage files you choose to delete
Photos & VideosTo detect duplicates and large media for cleanup

You can review or revoke any permission at any time through your device's Settings → Apps → Deep Clean → Permissions. Revoking storage access will limit the app's core cleaning features but won't affect anything else on your device.

07Data retention

Because Deep Clean does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no server-side retention period to disclose. Any information the app uses to display scan results (such as file size or duplicate groupings) is generated on demand and discarded when the app is closed or the device is restarted.

08Your rights

Since Deep Clean does not collect, store, or process personal data on any server, there is no remote profile to access, correct, export, or delete — that data simply doesn't exist outside your device. You retain full control over the files themselves at all times, and you may uninstall the app to remove all local app data.

If you have questions about how this applies to your rights under regulations such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact the developer using the details below.

09Security

Since all processing happens locally, your data remains under your device's own security controls (such as screen lock, disk encryption, and OS-level sandboxing) rather than a system managed by the developer. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date to benefit from the latest security protections.

10Children's privacy

Deep Clean is not intended for children under 13, and no personal data is knowingly collected from anyone, regardless of age. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a concern, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

11Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future releases to reflect new features or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page along with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
